# Understanding the QALY: Definition, Calculation, and Debates

A five-lecture course unpacking the Quality-Adjusted Life Year: what it means, how it is measured in practice, the welfare-economic theory behind it, and the ethical and empirical controversies that surround its use in health policy.

1. [What is a QALY, and why do we need it?](https://drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/dcast/understanding-the-qaly/what-is-a-qaly-and-why-do-we-need-it.yaml)
   - Why isn't 'extra years of life' enough to compare two treatments?
   - How does the QALY combine quantity and quality of life into one number?
   - What does it mean for a health state to be valued at 0.6 rather than 1.0?
2. [How is a QALY actually calculated?](https://drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/dcast/understanding-the-qaly/how-is-a-qaly-actually-calculated.yaml)
   - Where do the utility weights (u\_t) come from — who decides that a state is worth 0.7?
   - What are the main elicitation methods: time trade-off, standard gamble, EQ-5D?
   - How do we turn a health profile over many years into a single QALY number?
3. [The theoretical foundations: where does the QALY model come from?](https://drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/dcast/understanding-the-qaly/the-theoretical-foundations-where-does.yaml)
   - What assumptions from expected utility theory justify treating QALYs as additive?
   - Why does the model assume 'constant proportional trade-off' between quality and quantity?
   - What historical developments led health economists to adopt this framework?
4. [The ethical debate: whose life counts, and how much?](https://drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/dcast/understanding-the-qaly/the-ethical-debate-whose-life-counts-and.yaml)
   - Does maximizing total QALYs discriminate against the elderly or disabled?
   - Is it fair that a treatment extending a disabled person's life is valued less than the same treatment for a non-disabled person?
   - What do critics like the disability rights movement argue against QALY-based rationing?
5. [Does it work in practice? Evidence, limits, and alternatives](https://drawcast.app/#gh=hmelberg/dcast/understanding-the-qaly/does-it-work-in-practice-evidence-limits.yaml)
   - How well do QALY estimates agree across different elicitation methods and countries?
   - What real-world numbers do agencies like NICE use as cost-per-QALY thresholds?
   - What alternatives (DALYs, capability approach, healthy years equivalents) have been proposed, and why haven't they replaced QALYs?
